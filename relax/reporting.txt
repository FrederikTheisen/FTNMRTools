RELAXATION REPORTS — THREE FILES

Each analyzer run publishes three user-facing files:

  <peak-list>_intensity.pdf   plots and dataset assessment
  <prefix>_numbers.csv        one row per peak, for reading and analysis
  <prefix>_support.zip        saved fit and diagnostic records for audit/rebuild

The numbers CSV includes source collection/experiment and peak-list name,
assignments and coordinates, overlap/validity/warnings, the selected rate and
error, fit-quality statistics, and the all-points/shortest-delay-excluded rate
comparison when available. Every acquisition plane is included in original
order as delay, measured intensity, supplied noise SD and a fit-use flag. This
preserves repeated delays and gives downstream analysis the underlying values
without repeating long audit text on every plane row.

The PDF has one short summary, assessment charts, individual decay plots and
the rate overview. Fit parameters remain beside each decay, with the 80:20
main/residual plot-area split. Invalid fits have flagged raw-intensity panels.
NOE reports show ratios and mark exponential suitability not applicable.

The support ZIP holds the detailed portable report table, peak mapping and
warnings, selection audit, native fitter inputs/outputs and plotting data. These
support files are kept together instead of appearing as many separate files in
the experiment directory. The native fitter records are copied byte-for-byte.
The archive contains a short README. It does not contain spectra, peak lists or
CCPN projects.

REBUILDING A REPORT

From the experiment directory, rebuild the three files from saved fitter
records or the support ZIP without processing spectra, extracting peaks or
fitting again:

  python3 /path/to/relax_tools.py report \
    --saved-prefix 50mM_25C_600MHz_T1rho --experiment-type r1rho

For a portable rebuild, extract the detailed analysis CSV from the support ZIP:

  python3 /path/to/relax_tools.py report --csv /path/to/sample_analysis.csv \
    --output-dir /path/to/existing-destination

Only gnuplot is invoked for PDF rendering. Source inputs are checked for byte
changes during rendering. Existing reports are backed up, staged and published
with rollback if rendering or publication fails. A report refresh retains the
saved selection and never repeats the selection decision.

FIT AND DATA INTERPRETATION

Rates and uncertainties are the saved fitter values. The Gaussian goodness-of-
fit assessment uses the supplied noise SDs and does not rescale them. The
shortest-delay comparison reports both fits and the selected fit basis; it does
not establish a physical mechanism. Overlap and warning fields are review
flags. Repeated delays remain separate measurements. NOE ratios are not treated
as exponential decays.
